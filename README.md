Platinum Path — an offline guide and tracker for all 55 GTA VI PlayStation 5 trophies. Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade as PlayStation publishes it, plus a guide written in our own words that cites its sources, steps to follow, and a roadmap to the platinum in three stages: play the story (60–80 hours), finish the 100% Completion checklist (about 100 hours), then clean up the Strangers & Freaks strands and anything left over — about 200 hours in total, rated 4/10 by PowerPyx. The one trophy that can be missed, Positive Mental Attitude, is flagged, with the Chapter 1 meditation that may not come back. The tracker saves your progress in the browser, exports it to a file and imports it back, filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, side quests, robbery, exploration, money, and online — there are no online trophies), and has a spoiler setting that hides the story trophies until they are earned. The five trophies with no confirmed unlock method yet say so honestly instead of guessing. It runs entirely offline: open it with the network off and it still works.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Tick trophies off as you earn them; your progress is saved in the browser, and you can export it from the tracker controls.
